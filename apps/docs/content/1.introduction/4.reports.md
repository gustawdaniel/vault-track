# Reports and Analysis

VaultTracker provides powerful reporting tools to help you understand your financial health. Access the reports dashboard by clicking the **Reports** icon in the navigation bar.

## Report Types

### Cash Flow Forecast

Analyze your income vs expenses over time and project future balances. The chart shows cumulative cash flow, helping you spot trends and plan ahead.

### Monthly Analysis

Compare your spending habits month-over-month. See how much you spent each month and identify seasonal patterns in your finances.

### Categories Breakdown

See exactly where your money is going with a detailed category summary. The chart breaks down spending by category, helping you identify your largest expense areas.

## Filters

All reports support powerful filtering:

- **Date Range** — select start and end dates
- **Accounts** — include/exclude specific accounts
- **Categories** — filter by spending category
- **Projects** — filter by project
- **Type** — income, expense, or both
- **Exclude Transfers** — remove inter-account transfers from analysis

## Currency Intelligence

All reports support **real-time currency conversion**. You can view your entire financial portfolio in your preferred base currency (e.g., USD, EUR, PLN, BTC) with exchange rates updated daily from the backend API.

The Exchange Rates Viewer at the bottom of the Reports page shows the current rates for all currencies you use across your accounts.
