# Reports and Analysis

VaultTrack provides powerful reporting tools to help you understand your financial health. Access the reports dashboard by clicking the **Reports** icon in the navigation bar.

## Overview

The reporting section offers several key visualizations:

1.  **Cash Flow Forecast**: Analyze your income vs expenses over time and project future balances based on recurring transactions.
2.  **Monthly Analysis**: Compare your spending habits month-over-month.
3.  **Category Breakdown**: See exactly where your money is going with detailed category summaries.

## Currency Intelligence

All reports support **Real-Time Currency Conversion**. You can view your entire financial portfolio in your preferred base currency (e.g., USD, EUR, PLN, BTC) with exchange rates updated daily.
