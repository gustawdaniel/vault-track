
# Balance Assertions

Balance Assertions are verification checkpoints that help you ensure your recorded financial history matches reality (e.g., your actual bank statements).

Unlike simple categorization, **Reconciliation** is about data integrity. If your app says you have \$5,000 but your bank says \$4,950, your data is wrong, and any future forecasting will be inaccurate.

## How it works

1.  **Create an Assertion**: Go to an Account page and click "Add Assert".
2.  **Enter Details**: Provide the date and the expected balance (from your bank statement) for that date.
3.  **Visual Feedback**:
    *   The assertion appears in your transaction list chronologically.
    *   **Green Check**: The calculated balance at the end of that day matches your assertion.
    *   **Red Warning**: There is a discrepancy. The app shows the Expected vs. Actual balance and the Difference.

## Fixing Discrepancies

If you see a Red assertion, it means a transaction before that date is missing, duplicated, or has the wrong amount.

### Using the Reconciliation Widget

The **Reconciliation Widget** on the main dashboard automatically scans all your accounts for broken assertions.

1.  You will see a list of all failures across all accounts.
2.  Click the **Fix** button next to a failure.
3.  A pre-filled "Adjustment Transaction" dialog will open with the exact amount needed to correct the balance.
4.  Save the transaction to instantly reconcile your account.

## Best Practices

*   **Monthly Asserts**: Add an assertion for the last day of every month matching your monthly bank statement.
*   **Don't Ignore Red**: A red assertion means your history is broken. Fix it immediately to trust your data.
